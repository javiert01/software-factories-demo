# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, in their own words, to get their money back for a purchase. It is requested, then approved or declined by a person.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal, chargeback

**Refund**:
The money given back to a learner when a Refund Request is approved. It is always the amount the learner actually paid.
_Avoid_: Reversal, chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a purchase, counting the whole of the fourteenth day, in which a Refund Request can be raised.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a purchase can still get a Refund Request. It requires all of these: the purchase is inside the Refund Window, the learner has completed less than half of the course's lessons, has watched less than two hours of it, has no open Refund Request on it, and the purchase is not a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, refundable
